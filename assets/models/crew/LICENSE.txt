Two animated characters by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0
(Public Domain), downloaded from poly.pizza:

soldier.glb  https://poly.pizza/m/Btfn3G5Xv4
gunner.glb   https://poly.pizza/m/3hC2i0CTuO

Both carry the same skeleton and clip set (Idle, Idle_Gun,
Idle_Gun_Pointing, Gun_Shoot, Run, Walk, HitRecieve, Death, ...).

Guards wear soldier.glb, picked in the model booth on 2026-10-16.

Workers wear engineer.glb, built in Blender on 2026-10-16 by
build_engineer.py from those two CC0 models: the Soldier's body and
skeleton recoloured into orange coveralls, the Gunner's helmet head, and
added reflective bands, tool belt, pouches, wrench and air tanks. It is a
derivative of CC0 work and is itself released CC0. gunner.glb stays as the
source of its head.
