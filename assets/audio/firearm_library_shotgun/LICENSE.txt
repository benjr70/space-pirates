shot.ogg  the Shotgun's shot: a Winchester Model 12 at mid distance
          ("Model 12/K_17P.wav"), the first event of the file, mono 44.1 kHz,
          1.6 s with a 0.12 s fade, peak-normalised to -1.5 dB, then raised
          12 dB into a limiter at 0.84 (ffmpeg
          volume=12dB,alimiter=limit=0.84:attack=1:release=80). Cut by the
          sound booth's cut.py (branch prototype/new-gun-sounds, where it is
          audition_picks/shotgun_shot.ogg). One shot: no gap of silence in
          it, its loudness falls away from a single onset, and the two
          later blips (0.67 s, 1.00 s) last 10 ms each, reflections rather
          than shots. Picked by ear in the sound booth (new-weapons
          decision 12).
