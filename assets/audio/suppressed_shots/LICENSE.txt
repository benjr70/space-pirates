The suppressed shot of each gun that takes the Suppressor (the Weapon
Profile's suppressed_shot_sounds), picked by ear in the sound booth on
2026-11-24 (.scratch/weapon-attachments/issues/10-suppressed-shot-sounds.md).
Real recordings of a smaller or subsonic round standing in for the
suppressed gun. Each is the first event of its source, mono 44.1 kHz, 1.2 s
with a 0.12 s fade, peaking at -9.5 dB (8 dB under the unsuppressed shots),
cut by the sound booth's cut.py (branch prototype/suppressed-sounds, where
these are assets/audio/audition_picks/).

pistol_suppressed.ogg          Walther PPQ 9 mm, mid distance (Walther PPQ/X_31P.wav)
assault_rifle_suppressed.ogg   Ruger Single Six .22, near (Ruger Single Six/S_14P.wav)
marksman_rifle_suppressed.ogg  Savage 10 .300 Blackout, mid distance
                               (Savage 10 .300 Blackout/T_17P.wav), low-passed at 5 kHz
