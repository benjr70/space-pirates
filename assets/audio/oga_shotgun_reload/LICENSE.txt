shell_in.ogg  one shell going into the Shotgun ("ShotgunSounds/First
              Shell.mp3"), the first 0.7 s from its first onset, mono

              by the sound booth's cut.py (branch prototype/new-gun-sounds,
              where it is audition_picks/shotgun_shell_in.ogg). One shell:
              a run of small clicks with no gap longer than 0.05 s at
              -30 dB before its tail. Plays as each shell seats. Picked by
              ear in the sound booth (new-weapons decision 12).
