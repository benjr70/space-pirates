"Gun reload sounds", published on OpenGameArt under CC0 (no attribution
needed): https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds

shotgun_pump.ogg  the Shotgun's pump ("shotguncock.wav"), the whole file
                  from its first onset, mono 44.1 kHz, 0.44 s, peak-normalised
                  to -1.5 dB. Cut by the sound booth's cut.py (branch
                  prototype/new-gun-sounds, where it is
                  audition_picks/shotgun_pump.ogg). One pump: no gap of
                  silence in it until its tail (silencedetect at -30 dB finds
                  only the last 0.09 s). Plays 0.2 s after each Shotgun shot
                  and on the closing pump. Picked by ear in the sound booth
                  (new-weapons decision 12).

rifle_magazine_out.ogg  the Marksman Rifle's magazine ("gunreload1.wav"), cut
rifle_magazine_in.ogg   by the sound booth's cut.py (branch
                        prototype/new-gun-sounds, where it is
                        audition_picks/marksman_rifle_magazine.ogg: mono
                        44.1 kHz, 1.5 s, peak-normalised to -1.5 dB), then
                        split in two at its 0.4 s gap of silence: _out is
                        0-0.58 s (the release click, 0.05 s fade out), _in
                        is 0.60-1.5 s (the magazine going in and the slap
                        home, 0.01 s fade in). One take for the whole swap,
                        split so the second half sounds as the magazine
                        seats. Picked by ear in the sound booth (new-weapons
                        decision 12).
