# What the Shotgun is for

## Question

What is the Shotgun's role in the Sandbox against the Pistol and Assault Rifle, in ships built of Rooms, Lanes and Corridors? Decide its range band, shots to kill a crew member at each range, whether it is pump-action or semi-automatic, how many shells it holds, and whether it reloads shell by shell or all at once. What does it do in Aim Down Sights, and with a Slide or a Melee?

## Answer

The Shotgun is the doorway gun: the one weapon that kills in a single shot, and only up close.

- **Range band.** One shot kills a crew member (Health 4) inside about 4 m when most pellets land; two shots out to about 8 m; near useless past 12 m. The exact metres come out of [How the Shotgun's pellets fly](08-shotgun-pellet-pattern.md). The reason: the Pistol and Assault Rifle both kill in 4 hits and the Assault Rifle does it in 0.27 s, so a two-shot Shotgun would have no job, and since the Pirate always carries it, rarity cannot balance it.
- **Action.** Pump-action, with a long gap between shots (around 0.8 s, tuned in the balance pass) so a miss up close costs. Not semi-automatic.
- **Capacity.** 6 shells.
- **Reload.** Shell by shell, with a pump at the end when it was empty. Firing, a Melee or a weapon swap interrupts it and keeps the shells already loaded, unlike the magazine guns, where an interrupted reload gains nothing. This is a new reload mechanic added once as Weapon Profile fields.
- **Aim Down Sights.** Tightens the pattern a little and barely zooms (about 1.15x). The usual Descope rule applies. It does not make the Shotgun a mid-range gun.
- **Slide.** Firing during a Slide does not widen the pattern (the other guns widen by 1.5x). Sprint, Slide into the Room, one shot.
- **Melee.** Unchanged from the other guns.
- **Noise.** Loudness becomes a Weapon Profile attribute for every gun (how far crew hear its shot; today that is one constant for all gunshots), and the Shotgun is the loudest. One new field and one new sheet row.
- **Several targets.** One shot's pellets may hit and kill two crew standing close.
