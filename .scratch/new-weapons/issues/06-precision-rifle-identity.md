# What the precision rifle is

## Question

What gun is the precision rifle, and what is it called? Decide between a bolt-action sniper, a semi-automatic marksman rifle, a burst rifle or something else; its role against the Assault Rifle when most fights are a Room or a Corridor long; what it rewards (a headshot, a first shot, standing still); its magazine and reload; and how Descope treats it.

## Answer

The precision rifle is the **Marksman Rifle**: a semi-automatic rifle that rewards headshots.

- **Form.** Semi-automatic, one aimed shot per press, about 0.4 s between shots. Not a bolt-action sniper (a high-zoom power weapon wastes itself in 10 m Rooms and the Pirate would always carry it) and not a burst rifle.
- **What it rewards.** Headshots: one shot to the head kills a crew member (Health 4), two to the body. It out-kills the Assault Rifle only when aimed well, at any range, and it can drop an unaware crew member from outside their 14.4 m sight. Whether other guns get headshots is for [What the damage model needs](07-damage-model.md).
- **Hip against aimed.** Poor from the hip (a wide cone, about 3 degrees); near pinpoint in Aim Down Sights while still or crouched, looser when walking, bad in the air or in a Slide. The opposite of the Shotgun.
- **Magazine.** 8 rounds, a magazine reload like the Pistol and Assault Rifle, on the slow side (about 2 s).
- **Descope.** The same rule as every gun, with no special protection. It hurts this gun most, which is its counter.
- **Noise.** Normal gunshot loudness, the same as the Assault Rifle. It is not a silenced stealth tool; the quiet kill stays the Melee from behind.
- **Bolt.** The fastest and longest-reaching projectile in the Sandbox, so little lead is needed at Corridor range. Still a projectile, never hitscan. The number is set in the balance pass.

Zoom, scope or sights, and sway are left to [How the precision rifle aims](09-precision-rifle-aiming.md).
