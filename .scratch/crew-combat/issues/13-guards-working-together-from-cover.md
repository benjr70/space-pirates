# Guards working together from cover

## Question

"Guards in cover" settled what one Guard does. What do several do together?

- Covering fire: whether one Guard fires while another moves between spots, and who is chosen for each.
- Pin and flank, which today sends one Guard round through another Door while one fights: how it sits on top of cover. Whether the flanker moves spot to spot, and what it does on arriving.
- Whether "only one Guard moves at a time" stays the rule once covering fire exists.
- Suppressing the Pirate's cover when he hides, instead of waiting: who does it and for how long.
- Whether a Guard's gun decides its part (a Shotgun moves, a Marksman Rifle pins). "Where each gun stands" gave each gun a habit alone (Shotgun ambushes, Pistol shifts, Assault Rifle anchors, Marksman Rifle perches and never moves up); say here only what needs more than one Guard.
- Guards told of the Pirate by radio from another Room: whether they come spot to spot or run until they are near.

How many may hit at once is not decided here; that is "Gunnery that feels fair".

## Answer

Grilled with the user on 2026-11-25. Numbers are starting numbers.

**Covering fire.** A Guard changes cover only while at least one other Guard is up and firing at the Pirate, whether aimed true or as a deliberate miss. It waits up to 3 s for that. A lone Guard, or one with no partner able to fire, moves anyway.

**One mover at a time.** Still one Guard changing cover at once in a fight. The flanker is the exception.

**Flank.** As today: one flanker, through a different Door into the Pirate's Room, while another Guard fights, giving up after 20 s. Changed: its route ends at a cover spot inside that Door, picked by the normal cover rules, not a metre into open floor. Between Rooms it is exempt from the fair-walk rule, since it goes round out of his sight. It moves regardless of the one-mover rule and needs no covering fire. Giving up, it takes cover where it stands.

**Who flanks.** By gun: Shotgun first, then Pistol, then Assault Rifle. A Marksman Rifle never flanks. The Shotgun's doorway ambush and the flank are one thing: where his Room has a second Door, the Shotgun Guard ambushes at that one.

**Suppressing his cover.** When the Pirate breaks sight behind a prop during a fight, Guards who can see that prop keep firing at its edges for up to 4 s: deliberate misses only, never true aim. One Guard may move up under the covering-fire rule while they do. After 4 s they hold and wait, as played. Only Pistol and Assault Rifle Guards take part: a Marksman Rifle has no sight line without a target, and a Shotgun holds fire beyond 8 m.

**Guards called from other Rooms.** A Guard radioed in runs upright by its normal route until it is 18 m from his last-seen spot or enters a Room joined to his, then follows the cover rules and arrives spot to spot.

**The Alarm sweep.** A sweeping Guard that sees him drops into the cover rules like any other: it picks cover, radios and fights. No special behaviour.

**The last Guard.** A Guard that is the only one left fighting in his Room and the Rooms joined to it stops moving up, picks cover further from him and holds. It does not flee or surrender.

**Glossary.** No new terms.
