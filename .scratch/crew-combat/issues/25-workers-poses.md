# Workers' poses

## Parent

Part of `.scratch/crew-combat/spec.md` (Spec: crew combat). Build order 11 of 14. Vocabulary is `CONTEXT.md`'s. Numbers are starting numbers, tuned in the last ticket.

## What to build

See the spec's "How crew look" (Workers) and `issues/10`. Pose numbers: `git show prototype/crew-look:scripts/proto/proto_crew_look.gd` (`HIDE`, `HIDE_FLINCH`, `HIDE_SAT`, `HANDS_UP`, `HANDS_HIGH`, `KNEEL`, `KNEEL_LOW`, `BOUND_SAT`, `BOUND_KNEEL`), using the poser the Guards' poses ticket brought in.

- Each Worker rolls its own set once at ship build, from the ship's seed, even odds on each: hiding crouched with arms over its head or sat down; hands by its head or straight up; kneeling upright or on its heels; Bound sat against the wall or kneeling with hands behind its back.
- Hiding crouched, it ducks lower at each gunshot it hears, then comes back up.
- Hiding, its back is to the wall or prop it hides against and it faces into the Room.
- The surrender runs hiding, hands up, kneeling, as the prototype's sequence does.
- Bound sat, it sits against the nearest wall or prop; with none within 1 m it uses its kneeling Bound pose.
- The collision and head zone follow each pose.
- The stand-ins from the Workers and Surrender tickets go.
- `tests/shots.sh crew` (or a preset) shows all eight poses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] (agent) Over 400 Workers across seeds, each of the eight poses is rolled by between 40% and 60% of them for its pair, and the same seed gives each Worker the same set.
- [x] (agent) A hiding Worker faces within 30 degrees of directly away from the wall or prop behind it.
- [x] (agent) A crouched hiding Worker's head is at least 0.1 m lower within 0.3 s of a gunshot it hears, and back within 1.5 s.
- [x] (agent) In every pose the head zone is within 0.15 m of the head bone and the body's collision top is no higher than the head.
- [x] (agent) The screenshots show the eight poses on the Worker model with hands where the pose puts them (on the head, raised, or behind the back) and no defect from the checklist.
- [x] (agent) `tests/run_all.sh` passes.
- [ ] (human) Worker poses read as fear and as giving up, and three Workers in a Room do not look copied.

## Comments

Built 2026-11-26.

**Shape.** `CrewPose` now poses Workers too (`CrewPose.fit` no longer needs a `cover` for a Worker; `Crew3D.pose` is set for both). The nine prototype poses are in `POSES` with the prototype's numbers; `_target()` asks `worker_pose()` for a Worker. A Worker has no `CrewGun`, so its arms are solved in `CrewPose._arms`: each hand placed off the head, chest or hips as posed that frame (fields `arms`, `at_head`, `at_hips`, `hand_*`, `elbow_*`, `palm_*`, blended like the others, so a blend from hands-on-head to hands-behind-back is a straight blend of the two targets). The stand-ins (Wave looped, Idle_Neutral) are gone: SURRENDER and BOUND play Idle under the pose.

**Choices where the ticket left them open:**
- **The roll**: `CrewPose.roll_set(gen_seed, spawn tile)`, its own generator seeded `hash([gen_seed, "worker poses", tile])` (ticket 23's pattern, another salt), one `randf() < 0.5` per pair; on a layout with no ship seed it seeds off `_rng.seed`. No draw on the global RNG or `Crew3D._rng`. The four rolls are independent, as the spec says ("even odds on each"); nothing forces a Room's Workers apart (three Workers share all four with chance 1 in 256).
- **The prototype numbers held** on the real rig for every Worker pose, judged in the screenshots and by the head and capsule measures; none needed the deeper drops ticket 22 needed (the knee in the floor came from one knee's 0.60 drop with one foot planted; both kneeling poses put both feet back, toes down, and their knees sit on the floor).
- **Back to the wall**: the hide and Bound sat poses carry a `back` field; the hips and feet move back by `Crew3D.seat_back`, measured when it arrives on its tile (ray behind it at 0.45 m: back to 0.22 m off the wall, at most 0.3 m). The crouched hide moves back too, so its seat is near the wall.
- **Facing**: on its hiding tile the body faces `CrewHiding.facing` even with him in sight (before, it turned to him); it watches him by turning its head, at most 60 degrees. `CrewHiding._into_room` gave no facing on a tile between two solid sides facing each other (a slot); such a tile now faces along the open way most toward the Room's middle. The facing check leaves those out (`_has_back`): there is no wall behind.
- **Ducking** at every gunshot a hiding Worker hears, his or a crew member's (`Crew3D.hear`); only the crouched hide shows it (the sat one has nowhere lower to go). Held `FLINCH` 0.5 s, as a Guard's near miss.
- **Bound sat**: on being Bound it looks for the nearest wall or prop in 16 ways at 0.45 m within 1 m of its centre. Its body moves to 0.37 m off it (the capsule's radius and a margin) and turns away from it at once, so the Bound Worker's position never changes after the bind (ticket 24's "not moved 0.05 m in 120 s" still holds); the drawn model slides there from where it knelt over 0.5 s (a tween on the Model node's transform). With none within 1 m it takes `bound_kneel`.
- **The capsule** (`CrewPose.WORKER_TOP`, set every physics step by `Crew3D._fit_capsule`, on the Worker's own copy of the shape): cut under the middle of the drawn head in each pose; measured in Evidence. The ducked hide gets its own lower top for its 0.5 s. "No higher than the head" is checked against the head zone's centre (the middle of the head), not its top.
- **Hidden bodies**: as for Guards, the hips, spine and head are posed even when the ship's reveal hides the Worker, so its head zone is where the pose puts it; legs and arms only when it can be seen.

**Existing checks changed:** `tests/test_crew.gd::_chest` (where his crosshair rests on a Worker in ticket 24's surrender, bind and Door checks) was a fixed 1.0 m up; a crouched or sat Worker's capsule is now under 1.0 m, so the aimed surrender check failed ("his crosshair is not on the Worker's chest"). It now aims at 1.0 m or 0.3 m under the capsule's top, whichever is lower, as the smoke bot's `_chest` already does. Every assertion in those checks is unchanged and passes. `_check_workers_in_fight` gains the roll and facing assertions. No check was loosened.

**Fixed on the way, outside the ticket (it blocked the gate):** the first full gate failed `only=crew` on seed 208410349, `_check_workers_in_fight` (ticket 23): "at 25 s, Workers not within 0.5 m of a corner: a Worker of Room 10 in state 8, running, at (95, 99)". Replayed exactly, it fails the same on this branch's parent (`b19a5ce`, before this ticket), so it is ticket 23's. Cause, from a per-frame trace in the replay: two Workers of Room 10 ran into a one-wide dead end of three tiles, (95, 98) to (95, 100), entered at the middle; each ended on the way to the other's tile and pushed against it for the rest of the fight (`RUN_GIVE_UP` is 30 s). For that, `_taken()` now counts another Worker's tile only once it is hidden on it (a runner's target was never shared anyway: `find` skips it). The replay now passes (7 Workers hidden by 25 s).
