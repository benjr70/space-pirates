# Guards work together

## Parent

Part of `.scratch/crew-combat/spec.md` (Spec: crew combat). Build order 7 of 14. Vocabulary is `CONTEXT.md`'s. Numbers are starting numbers, tuned in the last ticket.

## What to build

See the spec's "Guards together" and `issues/13`.

- Covering fire: a Guard changes cover only while another is up and firing at the Pirate (true or a deliberate miss), waiting up to 3 s; alone, or with no partner able to fire, it moves anyway.
- One mover at a time in a fight, plus one flanker.
- Flank: today's rule, ending at a cover spot inside the far Door picked by the cover rules; exempt from the fair-walk rule between Rooms; needs no covering fire; on giving up at 20 s it takes cover where it stands. Flanker chosen by gun: Shotgun, then Pistol, then Assault Rifle, never a Marksman Rifle. Where his Room has a second Door the Shotgun's doorway ambush is at that Door.
- Suppression: when he breaks sight behind a prop during a fight, Pistol and Assault Rifle Guards that can see the prop fire at its edges for up to 4 s, deliberate misses only; one Guard may move up meanwhile; then they hold.
- A Guard radioed in runs upright until 18 m from the last-seen spot or until it enters a Room joined to his, then follows the cover rules.
- A sweeping Guard that sees him uses the cover rules.
- The last Guard fighting in his Room and the Rooms joined to it stops moving up, picks cover further from him and holds.
- The suppression time lives on the Sandbox resource.
- Update `CREW_AI.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] (agent) With two Guards in a fight, whenever one leaves its cover tile for another the other has fired within the previous 1 s, or the mover had waited 3 s, within 0.2 s.
- [x] (agent) Over a 60 s three-Guard fight, never more than one Guard is between cover tiles at once, not counting a flanker.
- [x] (agent) In a Room with two Doors and a Shotgun, a Pistol and an Assault Rifle Guard outside, the flanker is the Shotgun Guard, and it ends within 0.5 m of a tile hidden from the Pirate inside the second Door.
- [x] (agent) A Marksman Rifle Guard is never the flanker over 20 seeded fights.
- [x] (agent) After the Pirate breaks sight behind a crate, at least one shot is aimed within 0.8 m of the crate's edge in the next 4 s, none is aimed true, and none is fired at it after 4.5 s.
- [x] (agent) A Guard radioed from two Rooms away is on a cover tile hidden from the Pirate when it first comes within 10 m of him.
- [x] (agent) With one Guard left, its cover tile 10 s later is further from the Pirate than the one it held, and it does not come nearer in the next 20 s.
- [x] (agent) `tests/run_all.sh` passes.
- [ ] (human) Guards feel like they are working together: fire picks up when one moves, and hiding does not buy silence.

## Comments

Built 2026-11-26.

**Shape.** `scripts/crew/crew_team.gd` (`CrewTeam`) is new. There is one per Pirate (`CrewTeam.of`, meta on him), settled once a physics frame next to `CrewFire.update` in `Crew3D._physics_process`. It picks the flanker by gun (`consider_flank`) and runs suppression (`prop_between`, `suppressing`, `suppress_point`, `may_move_up`). Covering fire, one mover, the flank's last leg and the last Guard live in `CrewCover`, since they change when a Guard leaves its spot. `CrewCombat` gained `far_door` (the flank's Door, shared with the Shotgun's ambush) and `FLANK_ARRIVE` / `FLANK_SEE`. `Crew3D` gained `_start_flank`, `_end_flank`, `flank_done`, `_in_rooms_by` (the joined-Room takeover), `miss_at` (a suppressing shot, never true, `last_miss_kind` "prop"), `shot_frame` / `shot_age`. The Sandbox gained `suppression_time` (4.0) in its "Crew fight" group, so the sheet's Health and Melee table and `docs/sandbox.md` (regenerated) show it. Ticket 26 owns the real section.

**Choices where the ticket left them open:**

- **What counts as changing cover.** Every leave of a spot it held for another spot goes through `CrewCover._may_leave`: flanked, too close, hiding with no peek, two blind peeks, a habit shift, the ambush and the walk out of it, the Marksman Rifle's fall back, the last Guard's fall back. The criterion says "whenever", so the urgent leaves (flanked, too close) wait too. A Guard with a peek keeps its rhythm meanwhile (it peeks and fires while it waits). One hiding with no peek, seen, counts the wait in `CrewCover.waited`, so the smoke invariant on staying down in his sight leaves it out like a habit wait. Going from the open to cover, the first walk into the fight, and a flanker are not leaving a spot.
- **Covering fire** is a partner fighting him, up (mode SHOOT or OPEN), with its last shot within 1 s (`COVER_FIRE`). That is stricter than the criterion's "fired within 1 s". **Able to fire** (else it moves at once) is judged once, when the wait starts. It means a partner in the open, or holding or walking to a spot with a peek, not holding its fire, and with him in its sight now, or seen within 3 s while down behind its own cover or walking to its spot (it will be up or there shortly), or firing on his cover. Two versions were tried first. (1) Mode only, any MOVE counting as unable: a partner on its way to its first spot was "unable", and on seed 21 a Guard moved uncovered (`_check_covering_fire`). (2) Any partner with a peek counting as able: on the second `seeds=20` sweep (seed 951500065), `class=small ship=259611852 script=caught` reached no end state in 150 s. From the replay, one Pistol Guard stood up on a spot whose line to him had gone stale, never firing. Its partner waited the full 3 s for it before every move, as the bot shuffled round it, for 150 s. The sweep subset (`seeds=20 class=small script=caught`) passes with the present rule, and `_check_covering_fire` passed on eight seeds in a row. No prompt makes a partner pop up early: that would change every gun's time down, which ticket 18's rhythm check holds to the Gunnery.
- **One mover** replaces `mover_clock` / `MOVER_TIME` (4 s for a shift only). A Guard is the mover (`CrewCover.between`) from leaving a spot it held until it gets down, waits in ambush, or ends in the open. Every leave waits for no other mover, the urgent ones too, for the same reason as above.
- **Flank.** It is looked at on entering HUNT (as before) and every 1 s by the team. So a Guard already fighting from cover may be sent round; that is how the Shotgun of criterion 3, which arrives in the cover fight with the others, gets chosen. Candidates must be outside his Room, not have flanked in this fight (`flank_done`, reset on PATROL and SEARCH), not be the last Guard, and have a way round, with another Guard fighting to pin. Order: Shotgun, Pistol, Assault Rifle; on a tie one still hunting before one fighting (this keeps the old `_check_flank` meaning), then nearer him. The pinner for the Door choice is the other fighter nearest him. The flank ends within 1.5 m of the Door, at the route's end, or on seeing him within 6 m of the Door. It ends on a spot by the cover rules in his Room within 5 m of the Door, walked without the fair-walk rule (`CrewCover.begin_flank`). With none, it picks as usual. Seeing him further off it turns to fight where it is, as before.
- **The Shotgun's second Door.** `CrewCover.ambush_spot` tries the Door furthest from the Guard nearest him when his Room has more than one Door and another Guard fights. With no hidden tile in reach there, it tries the nearest Door (ticket 20's choice).
- **"Breaks sight behind a prop".** A Guard's eye ray runs at a fixed 1.5 m to his eyes at 1.5 m, so a crate (1.1 m) never breaks a Guard's sight, crouched or not. Read literally, suppression would never fire on a crate, against the criterion and the spec's "hiding does not buy silence". Changing crew sight to his crouched eyes would change Awareness, which the spec puts out of scope. So the trigger is losing the shot: every fighting Guard that had a shot at him (sight and a clear muzzle line) loses it in one frame, one of them having stood still (moved under 0.15 m, same mode, not stepping out or back), and a prop's tile lies within 2 m beyond him toward that Guard. A wall or a doorway frame does not count. Each shot is aimed 0.15 m past a side edge of the prop as that Guard sees it (alternating sides, no random draw, so no other check's random stream moves), at 1.0 m. Its eyes and muzzle line must reach that point. A suppressing Guard in the open does not step for a clear shot meanwhile. Suppression ends after the Sandbox's time or when anyone has a shot again. Suppressing shots do not walk in and do not count as a sighting's burst.
- **One Guard may move up** while they suppress: the first non-Marksman Guard whose shift finds a spot at least 1 m nearer, under the covering-fire rule (the suppressors are up and firing). It is recorded in `CrewTeam.mover_up`.
- **The radioed Guard** takes to the cover rules within 18 m or in his Room or one joined to it (`Crew3D._in_rooms_by`). "Upright" is a pose (ticket 22); hunting is already a full-speed run. Note: a Guard that enters the cover rules far off (a long joined Corridor) finds no spot with a peek within 8 m, hides, and searches after 14 s, as any Guard within 18 m with no lines did before. The cover rules have no "move nearer while hidden" step without a spot with a peek. This is not changed here; it may matter in the playtest.
- **Criterion 6's "cover tile hidden from the Pirate"** is checked as ticket 18's criterion 1 reads "tile hidden": the tile it stands on is hidden by the 0.9 m ray to his torso, and it is in the cover fight. In the rig the takeover that fires is the 18 m rule (it starts 18.4 m off); the joined-Room rule is asserted separately in the same check.
- **The sweep**: a sweeping Guard that spots him hunts and is handed to the cover rules at once (he is in its sight, so within 18 m). No code change; no dedicated check.
- **The last Guard**: a Guard that has had a partner fighting him (after him, in his Room or one joined to it, or flanking) and has none now. A Guard alone from the start is not the last Guard: otherwise every single-Guard check (bands, sight line, time to die) would fall back. A partner off flanking still counts, so the pinner does not fall back during a flank. It holds: no shift, no ambush, no flank. It takes no spot nearer him than the one it holds (in every pick, so being flanked never brings it nearer). It picks once a spot at least 1 m further.
- **Ticket 17's hook**: covering fire reads only the last shot; a reload adds nothing here.

**Existing checks changed:** none weakened. `_check_cover_fight_on_ship` gained the one-mover assertion (criterion 2). `tests/test_raid_smoke.gd`'s invariant comment now names the move wait counted in `CrewCover.waited`; its code is unchanged.
