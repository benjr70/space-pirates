# A habit per gun

## Parent

Part of `.scratch/crew-combat/spec.md` (Spec: crew combat). Build order 6 of 14. Vocabulary is `CONTEXT.md`'s. Numbers are starting numbers, tuned in the last ticket.

## What to build

See the spec's "A habit per gun", "Fair fire" (the two tells) and `issues/07`, `issues/06`.

- Range band and hold-before-shifting per gun from its Gunnery (Shotgun 2 to 5 m, 4 s; Pistol 4 to 8 m, 4 s; Assault Rifle 5 to 10 m, 8 s; Marksman Rifle 8 to 14 m, never moves up). The band is a term in the cover score, never a filter.
- A Guard fires at any distance with sight and a clear muzzle line; the engage range goes. The Shotgun holds fire beyond 8 m.
- Shotgun: may take cover within 4 m of the Pirate (no other gun picks a spot that close); too close does not send it back. With no fair walk into its band after 4 s it waits beside the doorway of his Room nearest to it, out of his sight and within 5 m of the opening, leaving when a fair walk appears or to search at 14 s. It racks audibly (the Profile's action sound) before each step out or pop up.
- Marksman Rifle: scores spots by the length of clear line to him up to 14.4 m and searches to 14 m; a perch one Room back counts only while the Door between is open and it opens none for the purpose; it does not move up when he hides or when out of band. With him inside 5 m it falls back to a further perch by a fair walk, else fires from the hip: doubled aim error, no sight line, may miss on purpose, needs no true-aim place.
- The sight line: a thin red line from the rifle to him for 0.9 s before each shot; the shot goes where the line pointed 0.2 s before firing. The rifle must hold one of the two true-aim places from the line's start to the shot, waits for one, and never fires a deliberate miss while perched. Hit during the line, it loses the shot and ducks.
- Update `CREW_AI.md`.

## Comments

Built 2026-11-25.

**Shape.** `scripts/crew/crew_habit.gd` (`CrewHabit`) is new: which gun a Guard carries (`gun_of`, by its weapon's file; a file-less copy is a Shotgun if it throws pellets, else a Pistol) and every habit number as a named constant. The habits themselves live in `CrewCover` (it holds the gun as `CrewCover.gun`), since they change its picking, holding and peeking. `scripts/crew/crew_sight_line.gd` (`CrewSightLine`) is the line's mesh. No number was added to the Sandbox or a Gunnery file: the spec's sheet sections (ticket 26) list no habit number, and issue 14 puts "everything else" in code. The band and hold were already Gunnery fields; they are now read.

**Choices where the ticket left them open:**

- **Engage range** (`Crew3D.ENGAGE_RANGE`, `engage_range()`) is gone. A flanker that sees him turns to fight at any distance; a Guard fighting in the open looks for a clear shot within its sight (14.4 m), a Shotgun within 8 m.
- **Hold before shifting** is the Gunnery's, counted while the Guard holds its spot in any of its modes there (down, stepping out, up, stepping back). Before, only time down counted, so every gun held a spot longer than its number by the time it spent up there. Re-claiming the same tile (after two blind peeks) keeps the count.
- **4 m.** Every gun but the Shotgun skips a spot within 4 m of him, the fall-back hiding tile too.
- **Holding fire.** A Shotgun with him beyond 8 m (where it sees him, else where it last did) does not peek, has no shot if up, and takes no true-aim place (`Crew3D.can_aim_true`).
- **The ambush.** Out of its band after its 4 s hold, a Shotgun first takes a spot inside its band by a fair walk if there is one, then its ambush, then the ordinary one-spot shift. With no cover at all it does the same after 4 s in the open. The ambush tile: in either Room the Door of his Room nearest the Guard joins, within 5 m of the opening, hidden from his eyes standing, nearest the opening first, the best six tried for a fair walk (21 m reach). It waits standing (poses are ticket 22), facing the doorway, in a new mode AMBUSH (state COVER). Seeing him within 8 m with a clear muzzle line it fires from there, the tile then counted as low cover so the cover rules carry on (flanked, too close and so on); seen beyond 8 m, it picks again. The ambush takes the one mover's place like any shift.
- **The rack** replaces the crew Shotgun's pump after each shot (`CrewHabit.racks`, `Crew3D.rack`): one pump a shot, before it. Over low cover it racks 0.3 s before its time down ends, still down, so the time down is unchanged (the ring's rhythm check still holds); from full cover it racks as it starts the step out, and the settle is cut to keep the shot within 0.95 s of the rack. Every shot (the open fight and the ambush included) is held to 0.2 to 0.95 s after a rack; an older rack is racked again and the shot waits 0.3 s.
- **Perch score.** The Marksman Rifle's score is near 20 (over its 14 m radius) + low 40 / full 20 + 40 times its clear line to him (from where it fires: the spot, or its step-out tile) over 14.4 m, less crowding; a spot whose line is longer than 14.4 m is no perch. With the other guns' six tries the longest lines were all across open floor in the band rig and it fought in the open, so it tries 18 (`PERCH_TRIED`).
- **One Room back.** No code of its own: a shut Door is on the world layer, so it blocks the sight and muzzle rays and a spot behind it has no peek. The Marksman Rifle opens Doors only on a walk through them, as any crew member.
- **Never moves up** is read as: no band shift either way, no advance when he hides, no search at 14 s while it holds a perch (the criterion's 20 s hidden needs this; the spec says it "stays until flanked or until he comes within 5 m"), and no re-pick after two blind peeks while he is hidden. It leaves when flanked or by the 5 m rule. Off a perch (in the open, or hiding with no peek) it searches as any Guard.
- **5 m** is judged from where it last saw him. The rule takes the place of "too close" (3 m) for the Marksman Rifle. The fall back is `find(-1)` (a spot at least 1 m further than it stands, fair walk); none, and it fires from the hip, looking again every 1.5 s, off the hip once he is 5 m off. Up with its line showing when he comes inside 5 m, it drops the line and ducks, so the next shot is decided down.
- **"Twice the aim error"** from the hip. The Marksman Rifle's Gunnery aim error is 0 (the spec's table: "0 (the gun's own 3 degree cone)"), so twice it would change nothing and the hip would be no worse. I read the aim error here as the spec's table does, the 3 degree cone the Guard aims with (the gun's base cone plus its Gunnery aim error), and double that: 6 degrees (`CrewHabit.HIP_CONE`, in `Crew3D.base_cone_degrees`). The criterion's check asserts the hip shot's base cone is exactly twice the perched one. If "aim error" was meant to be the Gunnery field only, this line needs a decision (a non-zero Marksman aim error, or accepting no penalty), and the check would change. Hip settle is 0.35 s (`HIP_SETTLE`), the ordinary guns'.
- **The sight line.** Its length is the Gunnery's settle (0.9 s, the spec's "settle 0.9 s (its sight line)"), not varied by 30 % (the criterion holds it to 0.1 s). It follows him (lead 1.0, so the full intercept) until 0.2 s before the shot, then stays fixed where the shot will go; the shot goes there (`Crew3D.aim_override`). The line runs from the muzzle point the crew already fire from (1.3 m up, 0.625 m ahead); ticket 16 may move it to the gun's model. The line's start counts as the burst start for the stagger, so the shot is never held past the line.
- **The true-aim place.** `CrewCover.wants_place()` is true for a Marksman Rifle (not on the hip) down with its time down spent, stepping out, up with a shot, or in the open with a shot. `CrewFire` treats that as wanting true aim (`Crew3D.can_aim_true`), queues it ahead of the other waiting Guards (`_sooner`), and never hands on or swaps a place it holds while it wants it (`CrewFire.pinned`). It waits down for a place, then pops up; it gives the place up after the shot, on ducking, or when it leaves. Queued first, it waits at most about one handover, so a fight of several Guards does not starve it. Hit during the line, it gives the place up, loses the shot and ducks (in the open, pauses for 0.6 of a time down).
- **`CrewCover.waited`**: seconds of one stretch down spent waiting on the habit (a place, or holding fire), so watchers timing a stretch down can leave it out.
