# Unarmed Workers run and hide

## Question

Workers carry no gun. What does a Worker do from the moment it spots the Pirate, before and after the Alarm?

Today: it flees to an Alarm Panel or a Guard, cowers one time in four, fires when cornered, and under the Alarm hides where it stands.

- Before the Alarm: whether running for an Alarm Panel or a Guard stays as it is, and what the one-in-four cower becomes.
- After the Alarm: where it runs. What makes a good hiding place (a corner, behind a prop, a Room away from the fight, its own Post), and how it picks one.
- What hiding looks like: crouched, facing the wall or the door, still or shaking.
- What it does when the Pirate walks in on its hiding place, short of surrender (which "What surrender means" decides): stay, bolt, freeze.
- Whether a hidden Worker ever comes out, and what it does when the fight moves into its Room.
- Whether Workers keep out of the cover Guards want.
- The new wording for Worker in `CONTEXT.md`.

## Answer

Grilled with the user on 2026-11-25. Numbers are starting numbers. Surrender is "What surrender means"; this is running and hiding only.

**Spotting the Pirate, Alarm down.** Three times in four a Worker runs for the nearest working Alarm Panel, else the nearest living Guard, as today. One time in four it freezes for 1 s, then runs to a hiding place; it no longer cowers on the spot. With no working panel and no living Guard, it hides.

**Once the Alarm is up.** Every Worker not already hiding runs to a hiding place, instead of hiding where it stands. A Worker on its way to a Guard switches to hiding.

**A hiding place.** A floor tile, by preference: in a corner (wall on two sides) or wedged between a wall and a prop; then out of sight of every doorway of its Room; then far from the Pirate and from any Guard's cover spot. It is picked in the Worker's Post Room if it can get there without passing him, else in whichever joined Room is furthest from him.

**How it runs.** At full speed. It refuses a route that runs toward the Pirate (the Guards' fair-walk rule without the open-floor limit), and avoids crossing between him and a fighting Guard when another route exists.

**Hiding.** Crouched, back to the wall, facing into the Room, arms over its head; still, not shaking; it ducks lower at each gunshot it hears. The pose itself is picked by eye in "How crew look in a fight".

**Found.** A hiding Worker the Pirate comes in sight of stays put and watches him. It does not bolt. What happens when he closes or aims is surrender.

**A fight in its Room.** It stays where it is. Stray shots can kill it.

**Guards come first.** A Worker never hides on a tile a Guard has claimed. A Guard may claim a tile a Worker hides on; the Worker moves to its next-best hiding tile.

**Coming out.** Alarm up: never, as today. Alarm down: after 20 s with no sight or sound of the Pirate it comes out On Edge and walks to the nearest working Alarm Panel (raising the Alarm on arrival) or the nearest Guard (alerting it to where it last saw him), then resumes its routine. With neither, it resumes its routine On Edge. This replaces today's silent return after 15 s: a witness left hiding now costs the Pirate his Ghost unless he deals with it or breaks the panels. The user agreed to this knowing it touches when the Alarm is raised.

**Wounded.** A Worker that is shot and lives always hides (no roll, no run for a panel) and never comes out, Alarm or not. Wounding a runner stops the Alarm without a kill.

**Traces.** Unchanged: a Worker that finds a body or the open boarding Hatch runs straight for a panel or a Guard with no roll to hide.

**Gone.** The Worker's Pistol, its Gunnery and fighting when cornered.

**Glossary.** `CONTEXT.md`: Worker rewritten as unarmed, running to raise the Alarm or a Guard or to hide in a corner, never fighting.
