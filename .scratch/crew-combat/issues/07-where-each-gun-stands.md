# Where each gun stands

## Question

How does the gun a Guard holds set where it fights?

With the cover rules from "Guards in cover" and the mix from "Which Guard carries which gun":

- A preferred range per weapon, as a band: how close a Shotgun Guard wants to be, where a Pistol and an Assault Rifle Guard settle, how far back a Marksman Rifle Guard stays.
- How a Guard closes or opens distance to reach its band, and what it does when the Room is smaller than the band.
- What a Shotgun Guard does when it cannot get close under cover: wait at a doorway, or push.
- What a Marksman Rifle Guard does when the Pirate gets inside its band: fall back, hold, or fight on with it badly.
- Whether a Guard will leave its Post Room for its band, and how far.
- How this sits with pin and flank: whether the gun decides who pins and who moves.

## Answer

Grilled with the user on 2026-11-25. Numbers are starting numbers, tuned in the Sandbox sheet. This sits on top of the cover rules in "Guards in cover"; only what differs by gun is here.

**Range bands.** Shotgun 2 to 5 m, Pistol 4 to 8, Assault Rifle 5 to 10, Marksman Rifle 8 to 14, as played in the cover prototype. The band is a field of each weapon's Gunnery. It is a preference in the cover score, never a filter: in a Room smaller than its band a Guard takes the best spot there is.

**One habit per gun.**

- **Shotgun: ambushes.** The only gun that may take cover within 4 m of the Pirate, and "too close" (3 m) does not send it back. With no fair walk into its band after 4 s, it goes to the doorway of his Room nearest to it and waits beside it, out of his sight and within 5 m of the opening. It never runs across open floor. It leaves the ambush when a fair walk into its band appears, or when he has been unseen for 14 s and it searches like any Guard. It holds fire beyond 8 m.
- **Pistol: shifts.** Holds the nearest good cover in its band and moves one spot when out of band after holding 4 s.
- **Assault Rifle: anchors.** The same, but holds 8 s before moving.
- **Marksman Rifle: perches.** It picks cover by the length of its clear line to the Pirate (up to its 14.4 m sight), searching out to 14 m instead of 8: the far end of a Lane or a Corridor, even one Room back. A perch in a Room one back counts only while the Door between stands open; it does not open Doors to make one. It never moves up, not even when he hides. It stays until flanked or until he comes within 5 m.

**The Marksman Rifle up close.** With the Pirate inside 5 m it falls back to a perch further off if a fair walk exists. If none does, it stays and fires from the hip: doubled aim error, no sight line. Hip-firing, it is an ordinary gun: it may fire deliberate misses and does not need one of the two true-aim places.

**Firing out of band.** The old engage range is gone. A Guard fires whenever it has sight and a clear muzzle line, at any distance, except the Shotgun beyond 8 m.

**Which Rooms.** A Guard fights in its own Room, the Pirate's Room, or a Room joined to either by a Door, and no further.

**Left to another ticket.** Whether the gun decides who pins and who flanks is for "Guards working together from cover".

**Glossary.** `CONTEXT.md`: Gunnery gains how far off a Guard likes to fight. Band, perch and ambush get no entries.
