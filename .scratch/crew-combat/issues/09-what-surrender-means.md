# What surrender means

## Question

A cornered Worker surrenders. What is a surrender, and what can the Pirate do with one?

- What triggers it: distance, being aimed at, being trapped with no way out, the Alarm being up.
- What a surrendered Worker does while watched, and when the Pirate turns away or leaves the Room: stay for good, or bolt for an Alarm Panel while the Alarm is down. The lean while charting was that it stays put while watched and bolts if left unwatched with the Alarm down.
- Killing one: what XP it earns (the lean was none), and whether anything else follows.
- Whether the Pirate can do anything else to one: nothing, knock it out, order it to stay.
- Whether a surrender counts toward Ghost, and whether other crew react to seeing one.
- Whether a Guard can ever surrender.
- The entry for surrender in `CONTEXT.md`.

## Answer

Grilled with the user on 2026-11-25. Numbers are starting numbers.

**Trigger.** A Worker surrenders when it sees the Pirate and either he is within 4 m, or his crosshair rests on it within 10 m for 0.5 s, and it is hiding or has no route away that does not pass him. A Worker still running with a way out keeps running. Guards never surrender.

**What it does.** It stands, hands up, facing him; after 1.5 s it kneels, hands on its head. It holds that for as long as he is in its sight. It does not move, shout or run.

**Left alone.** Surrender does not silence a witness. Out of his sight it goes back to hiding, and the rules of "Unarmed Workers run and hide" apply: with the Alarm down it comes out after 20 s to report; with it up it stays for good.

**Bound.** Holding the interact key on a surrendered Worker within 2 m for 2 s ties it up. The weapon is lowered meanwhile; taking a hit or letting go cancels. A Bound Worker sits against the wall and never reports or moves for the rest of the Raid. Only a surrendered Worker can be Bound: there is no binding an unaware one from behind. The HUD shows "Hold [interact] to tie up" with a fill ring within 2 m; a Bound Worker shows no Awareness bar.

**A Bound Worker is a trace.** Serious, like a body: a Guard that sees one hunts from there, a Worker that sees one runs for a panel. The finder does not untie it.

**XP.** Binding a Worker: 50. Killing a surrendered or Bound Worker: 0, and the toast says so. An unaware Worker kill stays 150; a running or hiding Worker not yet surrendered stays 100. The end screen lists Workers bound beside kills.

**Ghost.** Unaffected by surrenders and Bound Workers; only the Alarm counts, as today.

**Violence.** Shots and Melee work on a kneeling or Bound Worker as on any crew. A hit that does not kill leaves it kneeling. A Bound Worker killed by a stray shot leaves a body, a trace as usual.

**The Alarm.** Surrender and binding work the same with the Alarm up.

**Glossary.** `CONTEXT.md`: Surrender and Bound added.
