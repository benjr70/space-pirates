# Reference shooter numbers: shotguns and precision weapons

Research date: 2026-11-22. Facts only; this note recommends nothing for this game.

Question: how do arena shooters, Halo Infinite first, tune their shotguns and precision weapons, and what separates those roles from the automatic rifle's?

## How to read this note

Every number carries a source tier:

- **[O] Official**: a developer post or patch preview on halowaypoint.com (343 Industries / Halo Studios).
- **[T] Tag data**: Halopedia stat tables. For Halo 3 and Reach these cite the official editing kits' weapon tags. For Halo Infinite, Halopedia itself warns the figures are "derived from unofficial tools and may be inaccurate" (it says some were refreshed from the 2025-06-10 patch).
- **[W] Wiki prose**: Halopedia or the Apex Legends wiki (wiki.gg) article text, community-written, usually citing the game itself.
- **[G] Fan guide**: a single unsourced guide. Treated as unconfirmed.
- **UNCONFIRMED**: no source I could open states it. Left blank on purpose rather than filled from memory.

Sources that refused automated reading and so were **not** consulted: support.halowaypoint.com patch notes (HTTP 403), gamespot.com (403), apexlegends.fandom.com (402), bungie.net (empty page). Destiny 2 is therefore not covered. Search-engine summaries of those pages are not used as evidence, except where flagged.

Units: Halo tag data uses world units (wu). The usual conversion, 1 wu = 10 ft (about 3.05 m), was not re-verified in this pass. Apex uses Hammer units; the wiki supplies metre conversions.

### Rules that hold across the Infinite sandbox

- **Headshots only matter after shields break.** Every precision weapon's kill count is "N shots to pop the shield, then one headshot". A headshot on an unshielded Spartan kills instantly with the BR75, Commando and Sidekick. [W]
- **The AR cannot headshot in multiplayer.** Its multiplayer headshot multiplier is listed as none; a changelog line "2.55 >> 0" appears under the 2025 Spring Update. [T]
- **Everything is a projectile, with a short hitscan zone.** Tag data gives each gun a projectile speed plus a "hitscan range" inside which the hit is instant (AR 12 wu, sniper 20 wu). [T]
- **Shield and health point values: UNCONFIRMED.** The sniper's two-body-shot kill (first breaks shield, second kills) and the Commando's 8 + 7 breakdown suggest a shot's excess damage does not carry from shield into health, but no source states this.
- **Descope (being knocked out of zoom when hit) and flinch in Infinite: UNCONFIRMED.** No official or wiki source I could open states the rule.
- **Tuning levers 343 actually publishes** are mostly aim-assist terms, not damage: "Bullet Magnetism Angle", "Aim Assist Angle", "Headshot Prioritization Angle", "Bullet Magnetism Range" and "Falloff Range". The Winter Update nerfed the BR75 by shrinking these (magnetism angle 2.4 to 2.2 deg, range 20 to 18 wu) to "raise the skill ceiling on landing the final kill shot" and reduce hip-fire effectiveness at range. [O]

## What separates the roles

Stated by developers [O]:

- **The Bulldog exists so that "shotgun" is not a power weapon.** David Price (343): "We wanted to have a shotgun that was not a power weapon." It is "lower on the lethality scale of weapons and is thus more prevalent." Quinn DelHoyo: "fast-firing and fast-loading". (Inside Infinite, January 2021.)
- **Every sandbox item is meant to have its own purpose**; the same post frames shields as the core of the "dance", tuned by "max shield value, shield stun time, shield recharge time".
- **The BR75 is meant to be "the versatile weapon"**; when it over-performed, 343 cut aim assist rather than damage, to make the finishing headshot harder. (Winter Update.)
- **The Commando is tuned by trading handling against damage**: easier aim was followed by one more bullet to kill. (Winter Update, Season 3.)

Observable from the numbers above (comparisons, not developer statements):

- **Shotgun versus automatic rifle.** Halo 3 and Reach shotguns kill in one shot at point-blank and load shell by shell from a 6-shell tube; they are map pickups. The Bulldog gives up the one-shot kill (2 shots) and in exchange gets a 7-round drum, a whole-magazine reload and wider availability. Shotguns get no headshot bonus in Infinite, and Apex removed the Mastiff's. In all three games the shotgun's effective range is the shortest in the sandbox, set by pellet spread; where patterns are documented (Apex) they are fixed shapes.
- **Precision rifle versus automatic rifle.** The automatic rifle has the largest magazine and highest fire rate, the least zoom (none in Halo 3, 1.4x in Infinite), measurable spread (up to 2 to 3 degrees), and in Halo multiplayer no headshot bonus. Precision rifles fire slower, zoom further (2x to 3x), have little or no spread, and kill with a headshot once shields are down, so their kill count is a fixed "N shots plus one headshot" (BR75 4 bursts, Commando 9, Reach DMR 5, Stalker 3 headshots) against the AR's 16 to 20 body rounds. The costs differ by gun: burst cadence (BR), recoil and bloom (Commando, DMR), heat (Stalker).
- **Sniper versus precision rifle.** Snipers hold 4 rounds, fire about once every 0.7 to 0.8 s, zoom 4x to 10x in two steps, kill with one headshot through full shields or two body shots, and are far less accurate unzoomed.
- **Projectile speed tracks intended range** in Halo tag data: Halo 3 AR 80 wu/s, BR 180 wu/s, sniper 1200 wu/s; Infinite AR 360 wu/s, sniper 600 wu/s.
